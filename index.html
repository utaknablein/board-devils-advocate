Board Devil's Advocate

An independent AI challenger that tests every board proposal before the meeting, so directors walk in with the hard questions already on the table.

Challenges reasoning, never people Never recommends the vote Independent of the sponsor Same eight lenses, every time

See a challenge brief

Three fictional proposals, each challenged through the same eight lenses. This is what a director would receive alongside the board pack.

How it works

The challenger sits between management and the board. It reads the paper, not the room.

1

Intake

The company secretary submits the proposal when the board pack is locked. Management cannot edit the brief.

2

Challenge

Eight fixed lenses: premise, outside view, evidence, alternatives, downside, incentives, execution, pre-mortem.

3

Brief

Directors receive findings, the questions to ask in the room, and conditions a prudent board might attach.

4

Learn

Decisions and outcomes are logged. Over time the challenger surfaces patterns across this board's own history.

Where it goes: pattern memory

The enterprise product remembers. After enough decisions, it can tell a board how its own past approvals of this type actually turned out. The figures below are illustrative, to show the shape of the insight.
